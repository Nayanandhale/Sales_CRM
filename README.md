Udyogwardhini CRM
Transforming Leads into Admissions, Efficiently

Udyogwardhini CRM is a complete Lead Management and Admission Tracking System designed to help training institutes manage enquiries, admissions, counsellors, batches, webinars, and daily operations from one centralized platform.

The system reduces manual work, improves team productivity, and provides real-time insights into student admissions and counsellor performance.

Problem Statement

Training organizations often struggle with:

Managing hundreds of student enquiries
Tracking follow-up calls
Monitoring counsellor performance
Organizing admissions and batches
Handling webinar registrations
Generating reports manually

Udyogwardhini CRM solves these challenges through a simple and user-friendly platform.

Who Can Use It?
Administrators

Manage the entire organization from a single dashboard.

Counsellors / Agents

Track assigned leads, follow-ups, admissions, and daily activities.

View reports, performance metrics, and business growth analytics.
